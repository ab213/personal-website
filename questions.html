Questions Worth Asking

Ask one serious question.

This page is for questions with a little weight behind them: books, purpose, engineering, focus, health, ambition, and the things we usually only talk about after midnight.

If a question stays with me, I may answer it in a future post or conversation. New submissions are published right away.

What fits here

Not "hey what's up." More like:

  • What are you building your life around right now?
  • What do young engineers spend too little time thinking about?
  • Which books are worth rereading when your values start shifting?

Submit a question

Questions are stored in Supabase and published to the site right away.

Keep it direct. Thoughtful beats polished.

Add your Supabase project URL and publishable key in questions-config.js, then run the SQL in supabase/questions_worth_asking.sql.
